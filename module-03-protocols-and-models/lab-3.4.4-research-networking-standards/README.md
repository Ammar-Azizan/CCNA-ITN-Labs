# Lab 3.4.4 – Research Networking Standards

## Overview

This lab explores major organizations responsible for developing, maintaining, and coordinating standards used by computer networks, telecommunications, the Internet, and the World Wide Web.

The research focuses on the roles of standards organizations, important historical figures, networking technologies, Internet standards, and the importance of standardization for interoperability.

## Objectives

- Research major networking standards organizations.
- Identify the responsibilities of different standards organizations.
- Explore important people involved in the development of Internet technologies.
- Investigate networking and Internet standards.
- Understand the role of RFCs in Internet standardization.
- Reflect on the importance of standards for networking and interoperability.

# Research Findings

## 1. Jonathan B. Postel

**Question:** Who is Jonathan B. Postel and what is he known for?

**Finding:**
Known as "God of the Internet" as he made significant contributions to the developement of the Internet in particular to standards.

## 2. Domain Name System Management

**Question:** Which two related organizations are responsible for managing the top-level domain name space and the root DNS name servers on the Internet?

**Finding:**
- ICANN — Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers
- IANA — Internet Assigned Numbers Authority

---

## 3. Vinton Cerf

**Question:** What Internet organizations did Vinton Cerf chair or help found, and what Internet technologies did he help develop?

**Finding:**

- Co-founded the Internet Society (ISOC).
- Served as its first president.
- Chaired the International Network Working Group (INWG).
- Created the Internet Configuration Control Board, an ancestor of today's IAB.
- Worked with Bob Kahn on the design of TCP/IP and the architecture of the Internet.

---

## 4. Request for Comments

**Question:** What organization is responsible for publishing Request for Comments (RFCs)?

**Finding:**

The RFC Editor is responsible for publishing RFCs. The IETF produces many of the technical documents that become RFCs.

---

## 5. RFC 349 and RFC 1700

**Question:** What do RFC 349 and RFC 1700 have in common?

**Finding:**

Both RFCs document assigned Internet protocol numbers. RFC 1700 eventually replaced RFC 349 as part of the continuing Assigned Numbers series.

---

## 6. ARPAWOCKY

**Question:** What RFC number is ARPAWOCKY, and what is it?

**Finding:**

ARPAWOCKY is RFC 527. It is a humorous networking poem based on Jabberwocky that uses terminology from the early ARPANET.

## 15. RS-232

**Question:** What does "RS" in RS-232 stand for, and which organization introduced it?

**Finding:**

RS means Recommended Standard. RS-232 was introduced by the Electronic Industries Association (EIA)

## 16. SpaceWire

**Question:** What is SpaceWire?

**Finding:**

SpaceWire is a high-speed network and communication standard used to connect onboard spacecraft systems.

## 17. Internet Society

**Finding:**

ISOC — Internet Society promotes the open development, evolution and use of the Internet for the benefit of people worldwide. Historically, it has maintained offices in Reston, Virginia, USA, and Geneva, Switzerland, alongside a wider global presence.

# Reflection

## 1. Standards and Commerce

**Question:** How do Internet standards allow for greater commerce? What potential problems could exist without the IEEE?

**Reflection:**

Internet standards allow devices and services from different manufacturers to work together, supporting global trade and competition. Without IEEE standards, networking technologies could become incompatible, increasing costs and making communication between different devices more difficult.

---

## 2. Importance of W3C

**Question:** What potential problems could exist without the W3C?

**Reflection:**

Without the W3C, web technologies could become fragmented and incompatible. Websites might work differently across browsers and devices, making the Web harder to develop, access and maintain.

---

## 3. Importance of the Wi-Fi Alliance

**Question:** What can be learned from the Wi-Fi Alliance about the necessity of networking standards?

**Reflection:**

The Wi-Fi Alliance shows that networking standards are necessary for interoperability. Testing and certification help ensure that devices from different manufacturers correctly implement common standards and can communicate reliably.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Networking standards allow devices and technologies from different manufacturers to interoperate.
- Different standards organizations are responsible for different areas of networking, telecommunications, Internet architecture, and web technologies.
- Internet technologies are developed through cooperation among multiple international organizations.
- RFCs play an important role in documenting Internet protocols, standards, practices, and technical information.
- IEEE standards are important to technologies used in modern computer networks.
- Standardization helps provide compatibility and interoperability across networks and devices.
